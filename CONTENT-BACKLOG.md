## Geographic expansion sequence

1. **Toronto / Ontario high-value resources:** finish program-level details for current entries; prioritize pregnancy, housing, essentials, settlement and family wellbeing. Gate: actionable contact and accurate catchment, not organization count.
2. **Ontario-wide resources:** public-health units, EarlyON, childcare service managers, disability and Francophone supports beyond the GTA. Gate: provincial versus municipal scope clearly separated.
3. **Major newcomer destinations:** build one researched local pathway at a time for destinations such as Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton, Winnipeg, Ottawa, Montréal and Halifax. These are proposed research areas, not current coverage claims.
4. **Province and territory coverage:** verify health, education, documents, legal and settlement starting points for every jurisdiction. Include French-language and northern access considerations.
5. **Deeper municipal and community coverage:** verified neighbourhoods, postal coverage, delivery modes, accessibility, languages and maintenance agreements.

Every phase requires primary-source checking, explicit missing information, an actual checked date, a next review target and no implied partnership. Source changes or correction reports can trigger review before the target date.
