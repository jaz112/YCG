## What is this?

A way to turn “I don't know where to start” into one manageable next step. You do not need to understand every Canadian system today.

## Why does it matter?

Knowing the name of a service is only part of the picture. You also need to understand where it operates, who it serves, and how to contact it.

## What do I need?

Start with the province or territory where you live or plan to live, your main question, and somewhere to keep useful links. You do not need to share personal documents with YCG.

## What should I do?

Use the checklist below to organize your search. Open the government resources to find current information. Before using a service, ask about eligibility, costs, language support, and how to make an appointment.

## Where do I go next?

Choose one of the linked resources below. If you cannot tell whether a service applies to you, contact the organization directly and ask it to explain. This draft offers general orientation, not an eligibility decision or professional advice.
