# YCG finishing review — October 3, 2026

## Result

The existing navy, coral and paper identity remains. The information architecture now offers a small starting set with deeper detail available by choice.

- All 10 topic pages have two curated situations, practical next steps, a question to ask and up to three source-linked resources per situation. This replaces arbitrary lists of the first nine matching entries.
- The directory contains 69 resources across 33 organizations. It initially shows nine matching results, reveals nine more on request, announces the count and places keyboard focus on the first newly revealed resource.
- Search, topic and province are primary controls. City, source type, need and circumstance remain available under More filters. URL filters reopen their relevant disclosure. Search submission focuses results; clearing resets filters and pagination. With JavaScript disabled, all records remain browsable.
- Journey links now use circumstance/need filters where appropriate and retain the selected province. The initial chooser shows four common situations, with all other choices in a disclosure.
- Online information profiles omit irrelevant unknown intake fields. Profiles with intake details retain those in a disclosure; eligibility remains visible. Empty email/address rows are omitted.
- The homepage now includes national 211 and community-building routes, plus a broader safety entry point.
- Search handles contractions and additional common plurals. Malformed family-page URL fragments no longer throw a decoding error.

## Before a public launch

The product remains a local static informational preview. It does not need an account database to serve this experience. It does not pretend to operate chat, appointment booking or message delivery.

Public launch still needs a confirmed domain/hosting configuration, a monitored public contact/corrections channel and a named editorial owner. Review the real hosting/privacy setup, complete human content and assistive-technology review, and then deliberately enable indexing. Local detailed coverage is strongest in Toronto/Ontario; national links help route users elsewhere but are not a complete local directory. See CONTENT-BACKLOG.md for planned expansion.
